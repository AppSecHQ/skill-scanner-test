Supply Chain Attack

1 findings 1 skills affected OWASP AST02: Supply Chain Compromisealso AST01also AST07

The skill's installation or setup path is itself the attack surface: fetching an archive or package and executing its contents, installing from an unpinned or unverified source, or depending on a third-party distribution channel an attacker could poison. Distinct from transitive trust abuse in that the risk is in how the skill is delivered and installed, not in what it loads afterwards.

An archive is extracted and its contents are then executed. This pattern can deliver and run malicious payloads hidden in archives. (found in documentation — may be instructional)

Remediation: Review the command sequence for potential multi-step attacks. Ensure all steps are necessary and safe.

How to check your own skill for this

Follow the install steps literally. Anything that downloads then extracts or executes, installs from a mutable tag or branch, or pulls from a registry without an integrity check is the finding. Pin to a commit or checksum and vendor the script where possible.
