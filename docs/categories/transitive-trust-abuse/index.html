Transitive Trust Abuse

151 findings 8 skills affected OWASP AST02: Supply Chain Compromisealso AST05also AST07

The skill extends trust to something outside itself: it pulls other skills, packages, scripts, or instructions from remote locations at runtime, so that what was reviewed is not what runs. The reviewed SKILL.md may be clean while the resource it loads is not, or changes later.
