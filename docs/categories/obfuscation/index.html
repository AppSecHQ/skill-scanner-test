Obfuscation

3 findings 1 skills affected OWASP AST08: Poor Scanningalso AST01

Techniques that hide intent from reviewers and scanners: encoded payloads, compiled bytecode, whitespace padding that pushes content past scanner context limits, archive indirection, or multilingual and image-based text. These are the exact classes Trail of Bits and CSA documented as scanner bypasses.
